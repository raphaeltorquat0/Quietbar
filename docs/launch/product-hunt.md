# Product Hunt

**Name:** Quietbar

**Tagline:** Sony headphone controls in your Mac menu bar

**Description**

Change noise canceling, EQ, multipoint and more from the macOS menu bar, without the phone app. Native, open source and free, with no account and no network access. Tested on the WH-1000XM5, with experimental support for other MDR v2 models. Not affiliated with Sony.

**First comment**

Hi Product Hunt, I'm Raphael, and I built Quietbar.

[One or two sentences on why you built it, in your own words.]

It's a native Swift app that talks to the headphones over Bluetooth, so nothing leaves your Mac. You get sound modes, EQ, battery in the menu bar, global shortcuts and automatic profiles by app or Wi-Fi network.

It's early, so a few things to know. I've only tested on a WH-1000XM5, other models are experimental, and the XM4 and XM3 don't work yet. The app isn't notarized, so macOS asks you to approve it once.

If you own a different Sony model, I'd like to hear how it goes. Reports from real headphones are what move a model from experimental to tested.
